Jollof

Description

A recipe for making jollof rice. A staple Nigerian dish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of long grain rice
  • 1 large onion
  • Half a cup of tomato puree
  • 2 gloves of garlic and ginger
  • Bullion cudes
  • scotch bonnet pepper,red bell pepper and tomatoes
  • Half a cup of vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Put a clean pot on the stove
  2. Add the vegetable oil to the pot
  3. Add in your diced onion,garlic and ginger
  4. Add your tomato puree
  5. After your tomato puree has fried, add your fresh blended tomatoes,and peppers
  6. Now add your washed rice
  7. Add your seasonings
  8. Cover and wait till your rice is ready
