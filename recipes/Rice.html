Rice Recipe

Description

Rice is usually eaten with stew or sauces in Nigeria

Ingredients

  • Uncooked rice
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Spring onion

Steps

  1. wash your rice till water runs clear
  2. Add the washed rice to a pot of boiling water
  3. Add salt to taste
  4. Cook rice till soft
  5. Then garnish with spring onion
  6. Serve with your favorite stew or sauce
