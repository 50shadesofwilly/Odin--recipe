Vegetable Soup

Description

Vegetable soup is a popular soup eaten in Nigeria. It normally eaten with swallow like eba and fufu.

Ingredients

  • Pumpkin leaves
  • Water leaves
  • Protein of choice; beef,chicken or turkey
  • Palmoil
  • Habanero pepper
  • Onion
  • Seasoning cubes and salt

Steps

  1. Cook your protein till tender
  2. Add your palmoil to the protein broth
  3. Then add your pepper and onion
  4. Add your washed pumpkin and water leaves
  5. Add seasonings and salt to taste
  6. Let it simmer and serve
